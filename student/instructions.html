Examination Rules & Security Guidelines

  • Timed Examination: Once started, the countdown timer cannot be paused. When time expires, your answers will automatically be submitted.
  • Fullscreen Mode Required: You must remain in full-screen mode. Exiting fullscreen or switching browser tabs will trigger automated proctoring security logs.
  • Anti-Copy Controls Active: Right-clicking, copying, pasting, text selection, and keyboard shortcuts are disabled on this page.
  • Automatic Answer Sync: All your answers are saved continuously in real-time. If your network temporarily disconnects, answers are stored locally.

Camera Proctoring & Privacy Notice

TCIS Entrance Examination employs periodic automated webcam snapshot monitoring (1 compressed image every 5 seconds) to ensure institutional integrity. Images are stored securely and accessible only to TCIS Admissions Administrators for review.
